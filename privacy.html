Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Emberloop Android app processes, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

Your account uses your email address and may include a profile name and profile photo. You can add habits, schedules, goals, check-ins, notes, mood and journal text, focus-session records, reminder preferences, and app settings.

Where information is stored

Account and synced app data are stored in the Emberloop Supabase backend. Profile photos are stored in its private photo storage. The app also keeps account-scoped SQLite data and local preferences on your device so it can work offline. Local app data is not encrypted by Emberloop at rest. Signing out does not itself erase the local offline cache; deleting your account through the app requests removal of the account data and clears its local app data.

Notifications and photos

Habit reminders are scheduled locally on your Android device. A photo is chosen or captured only after you start the profile-photo flow; the selected photo is cropped and compressed before it is saved and synced.

Diagnostics and advertising

The current release configuration does not include a Sentry DSN, so it does not send crash reports. The code can support optional, filtered crash reporting in a future build; if enabled, the app provides a setting to turn it off and removes account names, email addresses, habit content, journal text, request data, and stack frames before sending an event. Emberloop does not include an advertising or analytics SDK in the current app dependencies.

Sharing and deletion

Exports and backups are shared only when you choose an option in Android’s system share sheet. You can request account deletion from Profile → Settings → Danger zone. The app asks you to type DELETE before continuing. See Delete your data for details.

Retention

Synced records are retained until you delete them or delete your account, subject to service-provider backup and recovery processes. This app does not currently publish a fixed backup-retention period. Local offline data remains on your device until you delete the account in the app, clear Emberloop’s app storage, or uninstall the app.
