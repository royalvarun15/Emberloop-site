Delete your data

Last updated 19 November 2026.

This page explains how to delete an Emberloop account and the associated app data.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the Emberloop account you want to remove.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.
  3. Review the warning and type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Keep the app online until it confirms deletion and signs out.

The request removes the account and its database records, profile photo in app storage, account-scoped local database, cached preferences, and local avatar files. Provider-managed backups may retain prior copies for the provider’s backup period.

If you cannot sign in

Email royal2004gamer@gmail.com and we’ll help with your deletion request. Do not include your password in a support request.
